About Fox3D

Purpose-built software for modern makers.

Fox3D is an independent South Australian software project building practical tools for the 3D printing community.

Why Fox3D exists

Makers lose time repeating searches, tracking materials manually and working around disconnected tools. Fox3D exists to remove that friction.

Our vision

To become the most trusted software ecosystem for the global 3D printing community by building practical, transparent tools that simplify every stage of the maker workflow.

Respect creators

Credit original designers and direct makers to the source.

Practical innovation

Build features because they solve genuine problems.

Quality over quantity

Prefer a smaller number of polished, reliable tools.
