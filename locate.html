Fox3D Locate

Know where every spool lives.

Locate is a barcode-powered filament inventory system for tracking reels, AMS slots, storage containers and unopened stock.

● In Development

Built around real storage.

Locate is designed for makers who have outgrown memory, handwritten labels and scattered spreadsheets.

When adding or moving a reel, Locate can recommend an empty AMS slot or container and explain why it was suggested.
Version 1

Planned core features

Barcode workflow

Scan to add, update, move or locate a reel.

Filament catalogue

Brand, material, colour, finish, weight, price, supplier, purchase date and notes.

AMS tracking

Track AMS-A1 through AMS-A4 and AMS-B1 through AMS-B4.

Container management

Track numbered storage containers with one-reel occupancy rules.

Bulk storage

Support unopened stock and bulk purchases in general Storage.

Search

Find reels by barcode, brand, material, colour, finish or location.

Move history

Record last-seen information and location changes.

Empty-location finder

Identify available AMS slots and containers and recommend where a new reel can go.

Future

Planned growth

  • Cloud synchronisation and mobile access
  • Low-stock warnings and purchase history
  • Print history and reel usage prediction
  • Multi-user support
  • Analytics and smarter storage grouping
